# Other mods in a session

Last updated 2026-11-04, for version 0.2.14. [Back to the start page](../index.html)

Every player in a session needs the same mods; FeverLink3 refuses a player who misses one, and names it. This page lists the mods that were active in our own sessions between two PCs over the internet, and what we saw. It is not a promise: a mod can change with its next update, and a mod that is not listed may work just as well. If something goes wrong with a mod, press "Report a problem" in the FeverLink3 window: the report names the mod.

How other mods are shared: what you click in a mod's own window reaches every game at the same moment, and what a mod does by itself is decided by the host's copy and applied on every game alike. Since 0.2.14 this also covers mods whose files are loaded by their mod id (most mods from mod.io); before, their buttons acted on the clicking player's game only.

## Works, with a known limit

| Mod | What we saw |
|---|---|
| [PYT - Pay Your Tolls](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/pay-your-tolls) | Played in a 74-minute session between two PCs (0.2.14-beta.2): its tolls, speed boards and toll income stayed the same on both games. The host's copy of the mod decides for everyone, so what it does in answer to another player's own build (for example naming a toll that player just built) may come later or not at all. Its pause and reload fixes (0.2.14) are seen in a two-game test on one PC only. |
| [Advanced Track Patterns](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/advanced-track-patterns) | Its junctions build on every game, but the game log says RESULT DIFFERS because the pattern stands as plain track; the worlds stay equal. |

## Fixed in 0.2.14, not yet seen in a session

| Mod | Status |
|---|---|
| [Industry Evolution](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3/m/industry-evolution-public-beta) | Before 0.2.14 a task taken in its window ran on one game only, and the games drifted apart when it finished. Its window is shared the same way as Industry Upgrades' since 0.2.14; a session with it is the next test. |

## Known issues not tied to a mod

- Upgrading a street station to a newer era with the button in its window can crash every game in a session. Until this is fixed, do not use the era upgrade buttons of stations while playing together.
